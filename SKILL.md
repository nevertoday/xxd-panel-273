## Panel-specific overlay

The transformed design is a small Art Nouveau emblem, not a full illustration and not a template logo. Reduce the subject to one memorable contour, then support it with a little plant curve, ring or geometric frame. Keep the field black, gray or warm white and isolate a single accent color taken from the photograph. The mark stays small. Type is a tiny elegant editorial word. Reject a complete scene, piled ornament, cartoon, a commercial icon and smooth decorative clutter.

These overlay rules add to `references/soldier-runtime.md`. They never replace the source brief or the family runtime contract.

Write every selected final PNG directly inside one fresh task directory under `~/Desktop/xxd/xxd-panel-273/` (or the explicit `--out` root). Use collision-safe filenames; do not create source, mode, or size subdirectories and do not generate an automatic contact sheet.
